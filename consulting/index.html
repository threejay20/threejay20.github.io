The problem

Leadership wants AI savings. The AI resolves a fraction of what was promised.

Customers get stuck in loops, agents clean up after the bot, and nobody can say whether it is paying for itself. The cause is rarely the model.

Knowledge nobody owns

The AI can only be as good as the help content behind it. Stale and conflicting articles mean wrong answers and escalations.

Contact reasons nobody mapped

Without knowing what customers actually contact you about, and in what volume, you are automating guesses.

Handoffs that make customers repeat themselves

When the AI hands over badly, satisfaction drops and handle time goes up, which cancels out the savings.

No one watching the right numbers

Resolution rate, service level agreement (SLA) performance, customer satisfaction (CSAT) and cost per contact need a baseline and an owner.

Questions

Common questions

Which helpdesks is this for?

Zendesk, Intercom, Freshdesk, Gorgias and Salesforce Service Cloud. If you use something else, ask; the method works on any system that can export ticket history.

Are you trying to replace my agents?

No. The goal is a blended team: AI handles the repetitive contacts well, and your people handle the ones that need judgment, with clean handoffs between them.

Do you need my engineering team?

Not for the diagnostic or the sprint. Integrations (for example, letting the AI check an order or process a refund) need an engineering contact and API access, and I build them myself.

What access do you need?

A ticket export for the diagnostic, plus read access to your helpdesk and AI configuration. For the sprint, admin access to the help centre and AI settings, with your approval on changes.

How is my data handled?

I work from exports or read-only access, remove personal details before analysis wherever possible, sign a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) on request, and delete your data when the engagement ends.

Can you guarantee a resolution rate?

Not before I have seen your data. After the diagnostic, I agree specific targets with you for the sprint and report against your baseline every week.
