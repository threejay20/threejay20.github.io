The problem

Most support cost hides in work nobody measured.

Volume grows, so you hire. Costs grow with it, service levels still slip, and nobody can say exactly where the time goes.

Repeat contacts nobody traced

Customers ask the same questions again and again because the cause, in the product, a policy or billing, never gets fixed.

Manual work between systems

Agents copy details between the helpdesk, CRM (customer relationship management), billing and spreadsheets on every ticket.

Reports that arrive too late

Service level agreement (SLA) breaches show up at the end of the week, in reports someone built by hand.

Tools switched on, not paying off

Self-service, macros, chatbots or AI features were bought, but nobody owns whether they actually take work off the team.

Questions

Common questions

Which helpdesks is this for?

Zendesk, Intercom, Freshdesk, Gorgias and Salesforce Service Cloud. If you use something else, ask; the method works on any system that can export ticket history.

Is this an AI project?

Not necessarily. AI is one tool among several. Many of the biggest wins come from fixing repeat contacts, removing manual steps and giving leaders real-time numbers. Where AI fits, for example answering common questions or helping agents, I set it up and measure it like any other change.

Will this mean cutting my team?

The goal is to handle growth without headcount growing at the same rate, and to give your people better work. Most teams redeploy time rather than cut roles.

Do you need my engineering team?

Not for the diagnostic. For integrations, for example checking an order or updating an account from the helpdesk, I need an engineering contact and API access, and I build them myself.

What access do you need?

A ticket export and your current reports for the diagnostic, plus read access to your helpdesk. For the sprint, admin access to the helpdesk and help centre, with your approval on changes.

How is my data handled?

I work from exports or read-only access, remove personal details before analysis wherever possible, sign a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) on request, and delete your data when the engagement ends.

Can you guarantee results?

Not before I have seen your data. After the diagnostic, I agree specific targets with you for the sprint and report against your baseline every week.
